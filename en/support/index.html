Frequently asked questions

Does the app replace my vet?

No. Patte Alerte is a personal record: it keeps what you enter and reminds you of the dates you chose. It gives no medical advice. For any health question, and in an emergency, call your vet.

I don't get reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings › Patte Alerte › Notifications. Also check that the care entry has a reminder date in the health record. A Focus mode can hide notifications.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

In the app, open Settings › Backup and export the file (to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop…). On the new iPhone, install Patte Alerte and import that file from the same screen. iCloud backups of your iPhone also include the app's data.

The “Call” button doesn't work.

Check that the number is saved (in the pet's profile for the vet, or in the emergency numbers). Calls don't work on an iPad without a phone plan.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the pets in the app, or simply delete the app from your iPhone: all the data it contains is erased. Remember the backups you exported, too.

Is it free?

Yes, the essentials are free and ad-free. An optional paid upgrade, Patte Alerte+, will add advanced features; its price will be shown clearly on the App Store. Subscriptions are managed in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Which animals are supported?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets, rodents, birds, reptiles, fish, horses, donkeys, goats, sheep, pigs, poultry, and “other” for everyone else.
