Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short
  • Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.
  • No account, no server, no ads, no trackers.
  • We don't receive, see or sell any of your data.

1. Who we are

The Patte Alerte app is published by Sofien Azzouz (“we”). For any question about this policy: gymindconsulting@gmail.com.

2. The information you save

Depending on how you use it, the app saves only on your device:

  • your pets' profiles (name, species, breed, date of birth, weight, microchip number, allergies, treatment, photo);
  • the health record, reminders, regular care, treatments, weigh-ins, daily log, walks (with their route) and observation journal;
  • the documents, photos and videos you add, stock, expenses and insurance details;
  • the numbers you save (vet, pet sitter, relatives…) and, if you enter them, your name and phone number (for the “lost pet” poster and the pet-sitter sheet);
  • your settings (language, preferences).

This information is used only to make the app work. It is never sent to a server: Patte Alerte doesn't have one, so we have no access to it. The app's files are protected by iOS encryption: they can't be read while the iPhone is locked.

If you save someone else's number (pet sitter, neighbour…), it also stays on your device; make sure that person agrees.

3. iPhone permissions

  • Notifications: for your reminders. They are local notifications, scheduled on the device, with no server.
  • Location: only during a walk you start, to draw the route and measure the distance, including with the screen locked until you finish it. The app never uses your location outside a walk in progress. The route is saved on your phone and sent nowhere.
  • Camera: to scan the health booklet or a document, when you ask. Text recognition (to find dates) runs on the device.
  • Photos: you pick the photos to import with Apple's picker; the app has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Calendar: when you add an appointment, Apple's add-event screen opens and you confirm the event yourself.
  • Phone: “Call” buttons open the iPhone's phone call screen.

The widget and Siri shortcuts read the same data, on the device.

4. What leaves the device, and only if you decide

  • Backup: you can export a file containing all your data and choose where to put it (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email…). The app does not encrypt this file: keep it somewhere safe.
  • Sharing: summaries (vet, pet sitter) and the “lost pet” poster are shared only when you tap Share.
  • Apple iCloud backups: if they are turned on for your iPhone, they may include the app's data, depending on your Apple settings.
  • External links: the link to the I-CAD website opens in your browser.

Once a copy is shared, it depends on the service or person who receives it.

5. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party data collection SDK. We don't sell or share any data. If you allowed it in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports; they don't contain your pets' information.

6. When you write to us

If you contact us by email, we use your address and message only to reply to you, and delete them once the conversation is over (at the latest after 3 years).

7. Purchases

Patte Alerte+ is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details or your identity: the app only checks with Apple that the purchase was made.

8. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can delete a pet and everything about it in the app, or delete the app to erase everything. Exported backups and iCloud backups must be deleted separately.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. Because your data is only on your device, you exercise them directly in the app (edit, delete, export a backup). For emails you sent us, write to us. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

10. Children

The app requires no account and collects no data from children or adults: everything stays on the device of the person using it.

11. This website

This website is a static page with no cookies, no forms and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process technical connection data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and secure the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

12. Changes

If the way the app works changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
