How it works

Three steps, that's it

  1. Add your pet

    Name, photo, breed, microchip and your vet's phone number.

  2. Copy their booklet

    Vaccines, deworming, visits: enter the dates from your vet's booklet, or scan it to find them faster.

  3. Get reminded

    A notification arrives at the right time. The next appointment also shows on the home screen and in the widget.

Features

Everyday care and the big moments, in one place

Health record

Vaccines, deworming, flea & tick, vet visits, weight and current treatments. Scan a page of the booklet and the app finds the dates for you.

Reminders on time

Notifications for boosters and regular care, a home-screen widget, add to Calendar, and Siri shortcuts.

Emergency numbers, one tap away

Your vet, the emergency vet, the poison center, your pet sitter: save them once, call them with one tap.

Summaries ready to show

A large-print summary to show your vet, and a complete sheet to share with whoever looks after your pet.

Documents, album and journal

Prescriptions, test results, insurance card, photos and videos, and a journal to note what you notice before talking to your vet.

If they get lost

Make a “lost pet” poster with their photo and your number in seconds, and keep the link to the French I-CAD registry at hand.

Also: walks with their route, a daily log (meals, walks…), food and care stock, expenses, a complete backup in a file you keep, and practical tips.

FAQ

Good to know

Does the app replace my vet?

No. Patte Alerte is a personal record: it keeps what you enter and reminds you of the dates you chose. It gives no medical advice. For any health question, and in an emergency, call your vet.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. If you change phones, export a backup from the app's Settings, then import it on the new one.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole app works offline, including emergency numbers and summaries.

Is it free?

Yes: for one pet, the essentials are free and ad-free (health record, reminders, emergency numbers, weight, lost-pet poster, backup, widget…). Patte Alerte+ adds more pets and unlimited documents and photos, for a one-time payment, no subscription. Everything is unlocked for free during the first month.

Which languages?

French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Arabic. The app follows your iPhone's language, and you can change it in Settings.

All questions and support

Patte Alerte does not replace a vet. In an emergency, call your vet or the emergency vet.
